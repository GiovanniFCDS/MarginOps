# MarginOps

**Commercial Performance & Workforce Analytics**

**Status:** In development

## Project Overview

MarginOps is an end-to-end data analytics project focused on analysing the commercial and operational performance of a hospitality business.

The project will use realistic synthetic data to explore how revenue, labour, food costs, wastage and forecasting interact to identify performance trends and produce actionable business recommendations.

## Business Problem

Hospitality businesses operate on tight margins and need to balance sales performance with labour efficiency and cost control.

MarginOps will investigate questions such as:

* Which periods consistently outperform or underperform sales forecasts?
* Where are labour costs exceeding budget?
* How efficiently are labour hours being converted into revenue?
* What factors are affecting gross margin?
* Where is wastage having the greatest financial impact?
* How accurate are sales forecasts?

## Dataset

The project will use synthetic hospitality data including:

* Date
* Site
* Forecast sales
* Actual sales
* Covers
* Labour hours
* Labour cost
* Food cost
* Wastage
* Budget figures

No confidential company data will be used.
